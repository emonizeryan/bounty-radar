# Product steering — Bounty Radar

## What this is
A single-page ranked board of free bounties for zero-capital hunters. It answers one question in seconds: "which free bounty is worth my time right now?"

## Who it serves
People like the squad's user: no capital, hunting free bounties (make.fun, bug bounties, hackathons, testnets), burned before by hidden fees and fake "free" offers. They need fee, payout, KYC, and eligibility facts BEFORE they click, not after.

## Non-goals
- No accounts, no wallet connect, no submissions, no payments.
- No financial advice. Scores are a screening heuristic from the squad's own framework, shown transparently — never presented as a guarantee.
- No fake "live" labels. Snapshot data is labelled with its date; only the make.fun fetch is live.

## Voice
Plain, honest, numbers-first. "Free" means $0 to enter. If a fee or KYC gate exists, it is on the card, not buried.
